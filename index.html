Our Craft

White is not a shade to us — it is a standard. Every shirt we make exists in exactly two forms: pure cotton and breathable linen. Nothing else earns a place in our workshop.

A Statement In Every Stitch

Two Fabrics.
One Standard.

Our shirt is cut in a single, considered silhouette — offered only in crisp cotton or softly textured linen. No seasons, no trends. Just the one garment, made properly.

A Deeper Look Into Our Craft

Highlights

One silhouette, two fabrics: a crisp 100% cotton poplin built for structure, and a softly textured linen built for ease. Both cut from the same considered pattern.

Design Philosophy

We chose to make one shirt well rather than many shirts adequately. Every seam, button, and collar point exists because it earned its place — nothing more.
